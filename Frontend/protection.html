Protect Your Brand

Investigate potential impersonation, analyse suspicious websites, manage evidence and protect customer trust from one private workspace. Every business gets its own workspace, and nothing is shared between companies.

Register Your Business Business Login

Brand Digital Twin

Record the brand's official website, accounts, support channels and purchasing channels once. Every investigation is compared against that recorded identity — and only that.

Threat Scanner

Four investigation types: social profile, website or domain, scam message, screenshot or image. Each submission becomes a persistent case with an investigation ID.

Explainable results

Findings show their source, what was observed and why it matters. Evidence that reduces concern is recorded next to evidence that supports it. Unknowns are listed as verification gaps.

Evidence Vault

Upload screenshots and documents, save addresses, record collection timestamps and attach everything to a case. Private storage, time-limited access links, no permanent public URLs.

Roles that mean something

Business Owner manages the workspace, membership and publication. Business Analyst investigates and adds evidence. Business Viewer reads permitted cases without making changes.

Trust Passport (opt-in)

Publish the approved official channels so customers can check what is genuinely yours. Publication is an explicit owner decision, and the verification status shown is the real one.

The workflow, end to end

  1. Register the business and complete the setup wizard (business details, brand identity).
  2. Record the official identity in the Brand Digital Twin, and optionally request verification.
  3. Submit a suspicious target through the Threat Scanner.
  4. Review the results: risk, confidence, evidence strength, supporting and contradicting evidence, and what still needs verification.
  5. Add evidence and notes, assign the case and set its status.
  6. Generate the investigation report from the saved record.

The workspace contains: Dashboard • Threat Scanner • Investigations • Brand Digital Twin • Scam DNA Tracker • Regional Scam Intelligence • Counterfactual Explorer • Network Mapper • Reports • Alerts • Settings.

What it will not claim

  • No threat-intelligence, registry or reputation lookups are connected, so no reputation score or domain registration date is shown.
  • Platform data that requires an account is not retrieved. Where a platform blocks retrieval, the case records the limitation instead of inventing content.
  • Nothing is published, reported or sent automatically. Complaint drafts wait for a human decision.
  • No security certification is claimed anywhere in this product.
