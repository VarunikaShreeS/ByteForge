About SENTINEL AI

SENTINEL AI is a digital risk protection platform with two separate experiences: a private workspace for businesses protecting their brand identity, and a free public service for anyone who wants to check something suspicious or report a scam[cite: 11].

What the analysis is based on

Public pages that can be retrieved safely, submitted text evaluated against transparent scam-language rules in English, Tamil and Tanglish, screenshot text read by an AI vision model and clearly labelled as an interpretation, and comparisons against the identity a brand itself recorded[cite: 11].

What it deliberately does not do

No threat-intelligence feed, domain registry, reputation service or platform trust API is connected. The platform therefore reports no domain registration dates, no reputation scores, no account creation dates and no ownership confirmations[cite: 11].

Nothing is submitted to a platform, bank or authority automatically. Complaint drafts are generated for a person to review and send[cite: 11].

Similar names, logos or handles are never treated as proof of fraud, and missing information is recorded as a verification gap rather than as suspicion[cite: 11].

Community reports

Public reports are submissions from members of the public. They are reviewed before publication, they carry the status that review assigned, and they are allegations or observations rather than findings of fraud[cite: 12].

Certifications and claims

SENTINEL AI holds no security or privacy certifications and publishes no customer testimonials. Any future claim of that kind will be stated here with its actual scope[cite: 12].
