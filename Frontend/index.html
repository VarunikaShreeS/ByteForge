How SENTINEL AI works

1

You submit what you have

A profile, an address, a message or a screenshot. No account is needed to check something suspicious.

2

We analyse what is actually available

Public pages are retrieved safely, scam-language rules are applied in English, Tamil and Tanglish, and a screenshot's text is read by an AI model that is labelled as an interpretation.

3

You get an explained result

Risk level, confidence and evidence strength are reported separately, every finding shows its source, and missing information is listed as a verification gap.

What this platform does not do

Stated plainly, because a risk tool that overstates itself is worse than no tool at all.

No threat-intelligence, registry or reputation service is connected. We do not display domain registration dates, reputation scores or account creation dates, because we do not have them.
A similar name, logo or handle is not proof of fraud, and a low risk result does not prove a target is safe. Missing information is recorded as a gap, never as suspicion.
Nothing is submitted to a platform, bank or authority automatically. Complaint drafts are prepared for a person to review and send.
Community reports are allegations or observations from members of the public. They are reviewed before publication and are not fraud findings.

Privacy principles

  • Business workspaces are private by default; nothing is published without an explicit opt-in.
  • Private evidence is stored privately and is never given a permanent public link.
  • Reporter contact details and screenshots are never shown in the public feed.
  • One company can never see another company's investigations, evidence or notes.
  • We never ask for passwords, OTPs, card numbers or bank credentials.

Why businesses use it

One private workspace for brand identity, investigations and evidence, instead of screenshots in a shared inbox.
Assessments that separate risk, confidence and evidence strength, so a decision is defensible when someone asks for the reasoning.
Reports generated from the saved record, so what was known at the time stays traceable.
A public Trust Passport that tells customers which channels are genuinely official — published only if the business opts in.
