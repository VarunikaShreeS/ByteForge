Privacy

This page describes what the platform stores, what it never shows publicly, and the separation between business and public data. It is a description of how this application actually behaves[cite: 23].

Business data

Every business has its own workspace. Investigations, evidence, notes, brand profiles, verification records and team memberships are associated with that workspace and are readable only by its authorised members[cite: 23].

Access is enforced on the data layer, not by hiding interface elements: changing a case identifier, workspace identifier or request parameter does not grant access to another company's records[cite: 23].

What is public

A business is listed publicly only if it explicitly opts in. The published record is a separate, sanitised copy containing only the fields the business approved: name, industry, logo, website, official accounts, support and purchasing channels, and its accurate verification status[cite: 23].

Internal notes, evidence, risk assessments, investigation history, employee details, private contact details and unpublished payment information are never part of that public record[cite: 23].

Customer submissions

A published community report shows the title, scam type, platform, the account or address reported, the description and the display name the reporter chose. Anonymous reporting is supported[cite: 23].

Reporter contact details and uploaded screenshots are stored privately and are visible only to the moderation team. They are never shown in the public feed[cite: 23].

Sensitive-looking content in a submission or comment is held for human review rather than published automatically[cite: 23].

Safety checks

A safety check shows its result to the device that ran it. For abuse prevention the platform stores a one-way fingerprint of the submitted target and the result category — not the text of a message you submitted[cite: 24].

Results are kept in the browser session only, so closing the tab clears them[cite: 24].

Evidence storage

Private uploads are stored in private storage. They are not given permanent public links; access is granted through time-limited signed links to authorised users[cite: 24].
