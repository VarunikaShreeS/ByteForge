What happens to your report

  1. You submit the form and receive a report reference and a private access code.
  2. Screening flags anything that looks like personal data or an accusation stated as fact.
  3. A moderator reviews it and publishes, asks for more information, or rejects it with a reason.
  4. If published, only the details listed in the consent notice appear — never your contact details or your screenshot.
  5. You can check the status at any time with your access code.

A community report is not a police complaint. SENTINEL AI does not submit anything to a bank, platform or authority on your behalf.

Privacy and safety of the checker

  • Never share a password, OTP, PIN, card number or bank credential with anyone — including this tool, and including anyone claiming to be from SENTINEL AI.
  • Look up a business's published official identity before you pay, and compare it with what you were sent.
  • A result of "No Major Warning Signs Detected" is not a guarantee that something is safe — it means no warning signs were found in what could be checked.
